## Operational details (fixed in code before any H-MS-002 number was computed)
The registry entry is brief, so these choices follow the H-MS-001 definitions:
- Primary pool: the six 1-hour main runs (`20261124T190304Z`, `200410Z`, `210516Z`, `220622Z`, `230728Z`,
  `20261125T000833Z`). The six ~60 s disk-budget probes are a **sensitivity pool only**. The 5-minute
  H-MS-001 capture is excluded because it was already used as evidence for H-MS-001.
- A capture is valid iff the replay has 0 integrity failures, 0 verification mismatches and < 5% unsynced
  events.
- Bootstrap: moving blocks of 30 s that never cross a (re)sync segment, 2000 resamples, seed 0, ≥ 30 blocks.
  Verdict rule: same as H-MS-001.
- Taker test: non-overlapping 10 s grid within each segment; trade sign(imbalance_5); cost per leg is
  10 bps fee + half-spread.

## Resyncs and excluded windows
Online, every capture logged 0 sequence gaps. Every resync was triggered by a WebSocket reconnect: the
capture invalidates the book and fetches a fresh snapshot.
- 190304Z: 1 receive timeout (10 s with no message), so 1 resync.
- 220622Z: 76 reconnect attempts, mostly DNS `getaddrinfo` failures and handshake timeouts. This was a local network outage, not a Binance gap. 3 reconnects succeeded, so 3 resyncs. Valid span 0.94 h.
- 230728Z: 5 receive timeouts but only 4 resyncs. The 5th reconnect came about 171 s before the end of the run, after the run had used up its **shared 30-snapshot cap** (5 sync + 25 verify). The last 1,699 events (4.9%) therefore have no synced book. They are excluded automatically, and the run still passes the < 5% rule. This was a capture bug; it is now fixed in `capture.py` with separate caps (60 sync, 40 verify).
- 000833Z: 3 receive timeouts, so 3 resyncs.

Offline, each reconnect shows up as a sequence discontinuity of 15 to 22 s. The replay counts these as gaps (1/0/0/3/5/3) and resyncs against the snapshot fetched after the reconnect.

No post-resync window needs to be excluded:
- All 163 verification snapshots matched the rebuilt book exactly: 5,807 untouched levels, 0 mismatches, including snapshots taken after resyncs.
- Horizon windows, bootstrap blocks and strategy trades never cross a segment boundary.
- The longest silence inside any segment is 0.205 s, so no stalled as-of mid is used.

## Descriptive: 10 s taker strategy (expected: does not survive)
2,095 trades on a non-overlapping 10 s grid.
- Gross mean +0.41 bps per trade (Newey-West t 8.3), hit rate 51.7%.
- Mean cost 20.0 bps per round trip.
- Net mean **-19.6 bps** per trade. **0 of 2,095 trades** were net positive.
- Even the average absolute 10 s mid move is only 1.52 bps (99th percentile 7.4 bps), far below the round-trip cost.
- **Does not survive** 10 bps taker fee plus half-spread. Answers to the registered expectation: no.

## Interpretation and limitations
- The spread is one tick 99.9% of the time. A 1 s AUC of about 0.9 is therefore largely mechanical: the mid moves when the thin side of the touch queue is depleted.
- The signal is statistically real but economically irrelevant to a taker.
- One night, one venue, L2 only. Bootstrap blocks within an hour are not independent of regime.
- Receipt time minus exchange time mixes latency with clock offset and is not used.
