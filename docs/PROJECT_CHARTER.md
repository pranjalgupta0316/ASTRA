# Project charter

**Goal**: an individual-developer, ₹0-recurring-cost research platform demonstrating institutional-style
quantitative methodology across US equities, Indian equities and crypto.

**In scope**: data engineering with provenance; statistical research; classical ML and PyTorch DL;
microstructure analytics on data actually available; portfolio construction and risk; event-driven
cost-aware backtesting; experiment tracking; optional local-LLM research assistance; Streamlit dashboard.

**Out of scope**: real-money trading; paid data/APIs/hosting; distributed infrastructure; claims of
profitability or production deployment.

**Constraints**: Windows 11, i7-14700HX (20C/28T), 16 GB RAM, RTX 4060 Laptop 8 GB, ~168 GB free disk.

**Principles**: research validity before complexity; real data; baselines first; reproducibility;
statistical evidence; cost-aware backtests; honest limitations. Status vocabulary: planned → designed →
implemented → tested → validated.
